Separate SSR finding
WorkspacePage calls loadUnifiedBank() for every authorized bank account before UnifiedWorkspace renders. That function fetches 12 full problem rows as well as curriculum metadata, including on home or exam-builder entry, although MathExamBuilder only consumes units. Avoiding those rows needs a metadata-only bootstrap and a first-page request when BankView becomes active; those edits cross WorkspacePage and BankView ownership. Finding and proposed boundary were sent to the coordinator; no SSR or shell files were changed here.
