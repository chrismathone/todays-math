  Full anchored templates consume all original conditions; no unit-based fallback exists.
  Source answers are recomputed from the original question and compared exactly before
  generation. Original solution text is not used as the answer oracle or copied.
  Coordinate homothety is applied only to parsed coordinates and affine constants.
  Section ratios, axis/reflection targets and translation amounts stay as authored.
  Finite population changes are specific to a completely matched counting template;
  explicit recipient lists must agree with their written count.
