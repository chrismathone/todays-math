## Expression and inverse-target deltas

An algebra→geometry representation delta needs an explicit map from algebra variables/relations to point distance, segment length, area or coordinates. Distinguish a segment/two points from its numeric length. Compare original and new admissible domains: a signed algebraic variable cannot become a positive length without recording a restriction and proving the intended method/difficulty still applies. Check signs, square/square-root branches, zero denominators, positive lengths/areas, coordinate location, units and required geometry knowledge; new forbidden prerequisites cause rejection.

For `M-m=R`, a direct-result→inverse-target plan may change “find R from M,m” to “find m from M,R”. Recompute `m=M-R`, **R≥0**, `m≤M`, and verify both extrema actually occur in the specified data/function/set and its domain. R=0 may be valid for a constant situation; R<0 is invalid. If the inverse instead asks for a parameter/input, enumerate every admissible solution and require the stated uniqueness or explicitly requested complete set. A formula rearrangement alone does not prove extrema existence or a unique parameter. Old answers are not a validation oracle. These are design validation examples, not produced/verified twins.

## Proposed separate twin dry-run / future load contract

### Artifact model

1. `ReferenceEvidence`: immutable paper identity (school/year≥2025/grade/term/exam type/subject), source paths and byte hashes, completed-pair selection/comparison, original question number and source digest. Reference original answer/solution/crops stay separate. Preserve exclusions and old evidence.
2. `TwinDraft`: stable new twin ID and version, original reference key, optional **confirmed** originProblemId, generator/template/prompt versions and seed; normalized content, ordered subparts/choices/tables, score/rubric; target mathematical facts/constraints; separately authored figures. Content has school substitutions consistently applied in stem, options, tables, image labels and solution.
3. `VariantDelta`: nonempty dimensions from `stem_structure|context|representation|inverse_target`, with combinations allowed; old/new condition organization, requested result/input/condition, algebra↔geometry mapping and/or situation/object/action/unit/diagram composition. Record before/after variable domains, signs, units, admissibility, given/sought roles and uniqueness requirement. Numeric deltas and school map are auxiliary. Each delta cites old/new spans or object keys. Invariants list common concepts, core steps, **CurriculumBinding**, Unit/live family and target difficulty. Nonempty descriptions do not prove sufficient change.
4. `FigureArtifact[]`: stable figure ID, role=`stem|choice|subpart|solution`, explicit owner/index, method=`v2|graph-1|elem-1|authored_image|asset_template`, spec/template/asset versions, asset-byte/SVG hashes, dimensions, target physical width, condition bindings, given/sought labels and review evidence. Original crops are reference-only and cannot be marked regenerated.
5. `ReviewEvidence`: separate math solver and verifier reports, **highest-priority whole-candidate grade/applied-curriculum review**, family review, same-principle review, **natural Korean review**, **distinct presentation review**, image/figure review and rights/similarity state. Curriculum reviews report per-surface checks and a legitimate student solution separately from verifier computation. Every result is `pending|rejected|verified` (or explicit unknown), with reviewer/session, evidence pointers and exact artifact digests. Independent sessions are necessary but their IDs and agreement alone are not proof.
6. `LoadPlan`: proposed Problem fields plus sidecar link; new `externalId` e.g. `exam-twin:<opaque-reference-key>:qNN:vNN` within 120 characters, no preset problemCode, `source:transformed`, confirmed origin FK or null, reviewed Unit/family/type/difficulty, new answer/solution/figures. Draft policy: pending, directUseAllowed=false. `readyForLaterLoad` and blocker list are distinct from DB approval.

### Digests and acceptance

Use three versioned, deterministic digests: **referenceDigest** (original evidence), **twinProblemDigest** (exact normalized stem/choices/subparts/tables/target facts/figure spec and bytes/ownership/score/grade-subject constraints), **twinSolutionDigest** (new answer/solution/rubric/solver version). Add **metadataDigest** (Unit/live family snapshot + labels/deltas/reviews). Known numeric and semantic typing differences must not be erased by hashing; define stable field order and explicit null/unknown. Original `problemFingerprint/v1` is not a substitute.

Solvers receive only the changed twin payload and recreated figures with its digest; original answers/solutions are excluded from their allowed pack. A separate verifier receives that same payload plus candidate solution, recomputes the answer and verifies every step, solution existence/required uniqueness, all choice truth values, units, diagram relationships, score and curriculum. Representation change can preserve the answer, but changing any bound field invalidates the old verification. Do not point existing `prepareRegeneration` at the original batch and claim twin verification.

Presentation review compares original and twin: retained principle/core method, real structural/context change, natural Korean, preserved difficulty and no forced everyday context on pure math. Include explicit negative controls: number-only, school-only, synonym-only, sentence-order-only, fluent text using a new prerequisite, and apparent context change with unchanged mathematical presentation. Natural Korean and distinct presentation are separate acceptance conditions; a passing similarity score cannot authorize rights or bypass math review.

The offline dry-run accepts explicit read-only evidence/snapshot inputs and writes **only its own new output directory**. Resolve physical paths, reject input/output overlap, traversal and symlink escape, and refuse overwrite of prior evidence. It does not instantiate Prisma, fetch a live taxonomy, copy to public/storage, synchronize an Exam, call any model, or have `--apply`/`--revert` functionality. Parent's fresh snapshot is injected with timestamp/hash; absent/stale/ambiguous bindings remain pending. At setup, all twin counts stay zero.

Admission **first rejects established grade/curriculum violations and holds unclear scope** across all listed surfaces; downstream scores cannot override it. Other checks include exact expected question coverage with explicit holds/exclusions, unique twin IDs, no original externalId/problemCode reuse, meaningful delta plus external presentation reviews, domain/sign/unit/existence/uniqueness checks, valid school map, complete answers/solutions/figures, exact asset array cardinality, current digests/snapshot/official-policy bindings, permanent exclusions, independent mathematical evidence and resolved rights state. No final readiness can be emitted while the external-origin provenance discriminator is unresolved. A future authorized loader must revalidate freshness/duplicates and preserve defaults/locks, with a separate write gate and load ledger; that writer is outside this worker's scope.
