Coverage and exact conventions
The family covers polygon side/vertex totals, length/capacity/area/volume
conversion, mixed-unit to single-unit conversion, length sum/difference and
broken line; radius/diameter; angle sum/difference/right/straight/full-turn
remainders, quadrilateral and plain isosceles apex/base; triangle, rectangle,
square, parallelogram, rhombus and regular-polygon perimeter with direct
missing-side inverses; rectangle/square/triangle/parallelogram/rhombus/
trapezoid area and directly computable side/height/diagonal inverses; unit
square area; cuboid/cube edge totals/surface/volume and direct inverses;
cuboid base-area/height forms; circle circumference/area from radius or
diameter and inverse radius/diameter.

All answers are canonical integer or terminating decimal exact values.
Decimal exact=[positive integer coefficient, minimum decimal scale]; integer
answers use kind=integer, exact=[integer string]. text and latex include the
same meaningful unit as answerUnit. Pi is always stated as 3 or 3.14, evaluated
as 300/100 or 314/100 using integer arithmetic. No floating approximations,
polynomial, surd or symbolic-pi normal form is needed. Square/cube/circle-area
inverses contain only positive integer roots in their finite domains; they
ask for the missing length, never introduce radical notation or equations.

Domains are genuine unique public tasks. No rotations, decorative variations
or identical givens inflate capacity. In particular, the cyclic quadrilateral
construction fixes one circular arc, leaving two independent parameters;
all displayed elementary angles are integers. Polygon groups are sorted
multisets of 2-4 separated 3-6 sided polygons (65 tasks per target). Every type
has at least 24 tasks and supports the ordinary 20-item request; larger
requests must respect its declared finite domain. Capacity limits are honest,
not random retry budgets. Root's sampler/regenerator owns count enforcement
and maximum-change selection over these finite unique tasks.

Concrete exclusions
1. 초1 has zero new types: the repository describes recognizing/making shapes
   and comparing length/area/capacity, not numeric geometry formulas. No
   grade-one formula drills or disguised ordinary arithmetic were invented.
2. Single-shape side/vertex recall, identifying names, constructing/moving/
   reflecting/folding shapes, measuring a physical drawing and proofs remain
   conceptual activities. Useful numeric early practice is the visible total
   of separated polygons. No visual rotation is counted as a fresh task.
3. Existing e4 triangle/right-triangle missing-angle types are untouched and
   not duplicated. Bisector/property variants, circumcenter/incenter and
   middle-school geometry belong to the other worker scopes.
4. Cylinder/cone/sphere general surface or volume formulas, sectors, general
   polygon angle-sum formulas, Pythagoras and trigonometry are not promoted
   into elementary grades. A feasible cylinder lateral-net rectangle example
   was observed at g6.ts:1447, but no additional cylinder type is shipped:
   root explicitly closed additional scope after accepting the 78-type,
   10,337-candidate census. This is a scope exclusion, not an assertion that
   the rectangle calculation is mathematically impossible for elementary.
5. Non-perfect-root inverse lengths, irrational answers, compound/composite
   regions requiring auxiliary constructions, nets requiring spatial
   deduction, hidden solid stacks and wordy application situations are out.
6. Time, mass and money are outside this geometry/length/area/volume scope.
   Mixed-unit answers with multiple blanks are not added to a scalar-answer
   contract; mixed given measurements convert to one explicit requested unit.
