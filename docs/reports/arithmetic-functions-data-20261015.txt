Implemented calculations
------------------------
Middle 1 functions: individual coordinates after reflection in x/y axes or
origin; point-to-axis / horizontal / vertical distances; direct and inverse
proportion value, constant and inverse input, including rational constants.

Middle 2 functions: function value/input and unknown coefficient/constant from
a point; slope from two points or changes; x/y change; x/y intercept; equations
from two points, slope+point and intercepts; parallel coefficient; translations;
implicit-line slope/intercepts and either coordinate of a two-line intersection.
Intersections are explicitly about function graphs, not generic system drills.

Middle 3 functions: general/vertex-form values; coefficient from vertex+point;
vertex coordinates and axis; general-to-vertex p/q; vertex-to-general b/c or full
equation; translations; global vertex extrema with the requested maximum/minimum
determined by opening direction; y-intercepts; smaller/larger integer x roots,
negative/positive radical roots, and tangency. Fractional vertices are included.

Middle 1 data: mean, odd/even median, uniquely determined mode and a missing value
from mean; frequency totals, relative frequencies, percentages, combined counts,
missing counts, inverse relative-frequency count/total; class midpoint; mean of
discrete values with exact frequencies; explicitly encoded stem/leaf median.
The frequency mean is for exact discrete scores, not a grouped-class estimate.

Middle 2 counting/probability: disjoint addition, product choices, distinct seats,
unordered pairs, digit cards with/without zero, exact coin heads and complement;
single-die thresholds/multiples; two-die sums, absolute difference, product
multiples, specific face and sum thresholds, each as counts and probabilities;
card multiples/unions; red/blue draws with and without replacement; three repeated
draws with exact red count; independent draws from different bags. All probability
prompts explicitly state equal likelihood and independence/replacement as needed.
No high-school nPr/nCr notation is presented to the student.

Middle 3 data: representatives/range, missing data from mean, deviations and
missing deviation; population variance / standard deviation of explicit lists,
including fractional means, zero variance and radicals; effect of ax+b on mean,
variance and standard deviation, including negative a and zero variance.

Excluded intentionally: qualitative graph reading/sketching, qualitative
correlation, numerical correlation coefficients, high-school interval extrema,
calculus, formal permutation/combination notation, ambiguous tied modes, and
probabilities with unspecified equally likely outcomes. Quadratic intersection
domains use shifted integer roots or centered signed radicals; additive shifted
irrational roots are not a generated form. This is broad direct-calculation unit
coverage, not a claim to every possible numerical or algebraic subcase, official
achievement-standard certification, or measured learning effects.

Exact answers and verification
------------------------------
All exact fields are canonical integer strings; no floating approximations.
integer: [n]
rational: [n,d], gcd(|n|,d)=1, d>1 (integers use integer kind)
polynomial line: [aN,aD,bN,bD] represents y=(aN/aD)x+(bN/bD)
polynomial quadratic: [aN,aD,bN,bD,cN,cD], descending degrees 2,1,0
Every coefficient is reduced with positive denominator; degree is exact.
surd: [n,r,d] represents n*sqrt(r)/d, r>1 square-free, d>0, gcd(|n|,d)=1.
Square radicands and zero normalize to integer/rational; n is signed. Standard
deviations require the nonnegative value; quadratic roots explicitly select the
negative/positive or smaller/larger value. Negative zero, nonreduced encodings,
wrong signs, display mutations and noncanonical exact strings are rejected.

problemAt uses finite enumerated domains and returns independent copies.
verify checks domain membership, the full prompt and canonical answer, then an
independent defining relation; it never calls problemAt or the solver. Probability
solvers use closed arithmetic counts. Verifiers enumerate distinct labeled objects
or ordered trials independently, keeping different balls of one color distinct.

Answer distribution: integer 38,812; rational 11,488; polynomial 10,136; surd 2,055.
