Arithmetic unnecessary figure/annotation audit — 2026-10-15

Owner request
Remove 겨냥도 wording everywhere in arithmetic display. Omit figures when
the complete public prose already determines the answer. Remove unnecessary
dimension/target-name/duplicate area annotations, including the reported
trapezoid bottom-side and cuboid base-area examples. Audit the full corpus.

Scope and decision
All 248 registered figure families / 17,552 finite variants were classified.
These are 248 types, not 17,552 different types. The variants are the finite
parameter combinations belonging to those types; source byte duplication was
not used as a type count.

Elementary: 84 families / 11,822 variants, all omitted.
Middle: 164 families / 5,730 variants; 153 families / 5,553 variants omitted;
11 families / 177 variants retained.
Total: 237 families / 17,375 variants omitted; 11 / 177 retained.

The two independent public-prose oracles reconstruct the answers of all
17,375 omitted candidates without parameters, SVG coordinates or family
solver output. The existing candidate answer is compared only afterwards.
Elementary additionally rejects 27,167 deleted-given and 11,822 deleted-target
mutations. Full finite tables and concrete reasoning are in the companion
elementary and middle reports. Unknown future families retain their figure
until explicitly reviewed.

Retained types and why
m1-parallel-angle: the angle-ray associations are visual.
m1-triangle-interior-exterior: C identifies the other remote interior angle.
m2-incenter-part-area: D lies on BC.
m2-iso-bisector-angle / base / half-base: D lies on BC.
m2-right-triangle-height / projection: D lies on AC.
m2-right-altitude-product / m3-right-leg-product: D lies on AC.
m3-circle-chord-length: M lies on chord AB.
Those incidences are not added to the original prose just to omit the figure.

Annotations removed from retained variants
92 complete numerical segment dimension groups and their separate numeric
texts; 66 chord measurement curves and 66 numerical-unit label groups;
14 duplicate parallel-relation notes. Chord dimension gap masks are removed
and their stroke masks cleared, restoring the continuous original geometry.
Point labels, structural geometry, right/equality/parallel marks and the
79 angle values needed for visual association stay unchanged. No generated
curves, inferred endpoints, layout optimizer or editor bypass was introduced:
the shared adapter only deletes the reviewed elements from trusted figures.

Shared display integration
Explicit family policies are consumed by arithmeticToStudioProblems and the
catalog compatibility renderer. Review, all print templates, saved-sheet
reopen, and admin paper previews therefore use the same projection.
When frozen elementary angle figures disappear, their original prose givens
are restored (the previous diagram helper intentionally suppressed them).
Instruction overrides remove obsolete picture/mark/empty-angle references;
the exterior-angle prompt drops 표시된. The prism condition 옆면은 모두
직사각형입니다 is preserved. All 겨냥도 wording is removed at display time.
Canonical IDs, prompts, parameters, answers, SVG bytes, snapshots, packs,
generation, regeneration, auth and save validation are unchanged. Required
saved SVG validation still runs even when the figure will not be displayed.
