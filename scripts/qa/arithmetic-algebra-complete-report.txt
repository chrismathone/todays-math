Finite domains and ordering
--------------------------
Domains use explicit small-integer Cartesian axes (rightmost varies fastest) or
filtered tuple lists preserving that order. No random retry, random sampling,
capacity clamp or arbitrary count cap. All indexes reject negatives, fractions,
nonfinite values and domainSize. Tuple construction rejects duplicate parameters.
Global visible-task identity uses the existing whitespace-normalized LaTeX plus
instruction contract, which does not collapse commuted/equivalent written tasks.
All new tasks are unique under that contract, and all 581,624 existing middle
numeric/polynomial/radical/supplement tasks were checked for cross-family collision.

Representative finite axes, with the exact full axes in the corresponding source:
numbers: factor integers 2..300; powers of 2/3/5; reduced signed fractions; recurring
  periods of length one/two; proper reduced denominator 2..100 fractions.
expressions: signed bounded integer coefficients and substitutions; positive
  integer exponent axes; no negative exponents or zero divisor variables.
linear: nonzero coefficients with A!=C for both-sides equations; positive explicit
  denominators; signed tenths handled as integer-scaled equations; 2-variable
  coefficient axes -2,-1,1,2 with nonzero determinant; substitution A+C!=0.
factorization: primitive linear factors; sorted factor pairs; perfect-square and
  difference-square cases separated from ordinary monic/nonmonic factor forms.
quadratic: finite ordered integer/fraction factor pairs, nonnegative square-method
  right sides, bounded positive leading coefficients and positive nonsquare
  discriminants for the formula family. No unknown infinite solution sets.
The full manifest, source domains and exhaustive identity checks determine counts.

Exact tuple contracts (no shared schema/kind change)
--------------------------------------------------
All fields below are canonical decimal integer strings; zero is "0", not "-0".
Production verifier bounds exact arrays to 30 fields and strings to 40 characters,
parameters to 6 safe integers. Numerical rational denominators must be positive,
and fractions reduced. Display text/LaTeX must be the exact canonical formatting.
Unknown answer/prompt fields, extra/omitted fields, sparse arrays and malformed
numbers fail. Property insertion order is irrelevant. No float tolerance is used.

integer/rational: existing [value] / [numerator,positiveDenominator].
Prime factorization (polynomial): [prime,exponent,...], ascending distinct primes,
  positive exponents. Check primality and reconstruct the original integer.
Expanded bivariate polynomial (polynomial): [xDegree,yDegree,coefficient,...],
  descending lexicographic powers; zero terms omitted. Empty list is zero.
Mixed monomial (polynomial): [xDegree,yDegree,numerator,denominator].
Inequality (polynomial): [relation,numerator,denominator], relation codes
  0=less, 1=less-or-equal, 2=greater, 3=greater-or-equal. Reader checks the endpoint
  and original inequality on both sides and at equality; negative division cannot
  retain the wrong relation. Displays use Unicode relations / LaTeX commands,
  never schema-forbidden ASCII angle brackets.
System (polynomial): [xNumerator,xDenominator,yNumerator,yDenominator], x first.
  Both original equations must hold and domain determinant is nonzero. Swapping
  distinct coordinates or scaling a rational pair is rejected.
Factored polynomial (polynomial):
  [content,xPower,yPower,factorCount,...factorCount*[a,b,c,multiplicity]]
  means content*x^xPower*y^yPower*product((a*x+b*y+c)^multiplicity).
  Content is nonzero, factor leading sign positive (x before y), factors primitive,
  strict lexicographic (a,b,c) order, repeated factors merged. Standalone x/y
  factors belong only to xPower/yPower. All factors are linear, thus irreducible;
  their product is checked with an independent coefficient convolution against
  the given expanded polynomial. An expanded answer or nonprimitive factorization
  with the same value is not a canonical answer.
Full quadratic real-root set (surd):
  [count,...count*[p,q,r,s]], each root (p+q*sqrt(r))/s, s positive.
  Rational roots use q=r=0 and gcd(p,s)=1. Irrational roots require squarefree r>1,
  q nonzero and gcd(p,q,s)=1. Distinct roots ascend; a repeated root appears once.
  A quadratic's irrational pair must be ordered conjugates. The verifier checks
  each root in the original equation over exact rational/radical coefficients.
  A singleton must also have zero derivative (double root); pairs must satisfy
  degree-two sum/product completeness identities. These are internal verification
  identities, not student-facing high-school relation exercises. Missing, repeated,
  reversed, unsimplified or unreduced equivalent root sets are rejected.
