Canonical exact values and verification
--------------------------------------
Reuse the existing geometry conventions: integer [n], rational [n,d], surd [n,r,d]
meaning n*sqrt(r)/d. Rational denominators exceed one (integers have integer kind);
all denominators positive; gcd(n,d)=1; r squarefree >1; zero is integer [0] only.
Read before arithmetic: canonical integer strings, max 40 characters/field,
max 3 fields and safe integer values; at most 6 safe-integer parameters.
Malformed, sparse, extra, oversized, unreduced, squareful or zero-denominator
representations fail, even when their display is regenerated coherently.
Equivalent reordered object keys remain accepted. Extra answer/prompt keys fail.

The generator reuses existing geometry rational/radical normalization and answer
formatting. The verifier does not call solve/problemAt or normalize an expected
answer. It checks exact inverse side-ratio/area relations and Pythagorean squared
ratios using BigInt cross-products. Every geometric length/ratio must be positive,
so squaring cannot admit the wrong sign. Only explicit special-square subtraction
can yield a negative answer. Inverse-angle verification compares independent
30/45/60 right-triangle side squares within the stated acute domain.
