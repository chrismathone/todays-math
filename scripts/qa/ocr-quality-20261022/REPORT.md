- OCR transcribes original-language stems, options, conditions, tables, units and
  printed answers. Newly authored explanations and warnings remain Korean. The
  blanket Korean instruction and the Korean-prose block definition were fixed.
- Native OCR keeps the existing storage contract and missing-choice safeguard.
  Four entries, including an explicitly printed four-choice source, still carry

  asserting a missing fifth. No fifth is generated by deterministic code.
- Non-five choice layouts request `auto`. The final crop prompt distinguishes
  short printed answer markers from large solution-workspace regions.
- A bounded native-image check compares source ink with the union of model crop
  boxes and their overlaps. It detects unassigned ink, boundary ink and shared
  ink; it never expands or repairs a box. Headers, answer keys, noise and
  handwriting may also trigger it. All nonempty crop candidates retain an
  explicit semantic-review advisory because pixel coverage cannot establish
  question ownership or detect a fully swallowed answer key.
- `useStudioAi` records page warnings before the empty-result branch. Existing
  `StudioQueueStatus` renders them; subsequent child OCR cannot overwrite the
  page job. Orientation feedback appends same-pass warnings. A successful
  rotation clears warnings from the discarded render before the next request;
  declined or throwing rotations retain the original-source warnings.
- Anthropic accepts text with valid thinking/redacted-thinking block types and
  rejects mixed tool, unknown or malformed text blocks even at `end_turn`.
  Other vendors were not changed.
- A final OCR-only safety advisory covers legacy generic replies that omit native
  OCR metadata. It asks for original text, option numbering/content and total
  count verification without fabricating metadata or completeness flags.
  Existing crop warnings render it in the review pane.

The page warnings are advisory, not a new storage flag or enforced import gate.

  hallucination. The frozen expectations and their failures remain unchanged;
  no parser or normalizer inserted those raw entries.

expectations, raw pointers, statuses, issue lists and pixel metrics are unchanged
after the final advisory. Five legacy replies now expose an explicit source-check
warning; all native-contract failures remain failures. Original parsed artifacts
were not overwritten.

Complete four-choice support is therefore unresolved under the unchanged
storage contract. Image reconstruction and semantic source completeness remain
unresolved. A correctly numbered five-entry result also is not pixel proof of
completeness. Warnings may be frequent; current source-ink checks intentionally
prefer review over guessing ownership.
