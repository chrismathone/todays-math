## Perspective semantics and independent counterexample plan

The requested true flag intentionally allows a nonorthogonal paper projection to display a projected right-angle marker. It must NOT be used as evidence that the two screen rays are perpendicular, nor prove spatial orthogonality absent a3D relation/projection certificate. Preserve ordinary planar right-angle verification when perspective is false/omitted; nonboolean and perspective-without-right must reject. The prepared true45degree case has a nonzero screen dot product: rendering success is a feature result, mathematical orthogonality approval remains unknown. Include actual perpendicular, coincident and collinear cases to detect unintended loss of unrelated degeneracy checks. No need to alter the six requested expected bytes to keep this distinction explicit.

Other prepared cases cover forged known-slug/nested/malicious asset groups, unit-variable opposition (2m,30 m,g/h,math mode,Litre variants and special spaces), and seven known geometry-shaped assets that must refuse stock substitution. Metadata safety, editor opacity and body provenance are separate boundaries; patch06 does not include src/editor patches.
